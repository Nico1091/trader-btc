TREND FOLLOWIN G  STRATEGY ANALYTICAL RULES Identify the major trend of the market from a higher timeframe (1M, 1W, 1D , 4H ). Draw trend lines. Map out major and minor support and resistance zones. Find trend pattern on a lower timeframe ( 15M,  1H, 4H, and 1D). Use the Fibonacci tool to identify points of possible retracements and wait for prices to trade in that region. Identify points of high confluence between the Fibonacci, support and resistance zones and your trend lines. Use high timeframes to  find candle stick pattern confirmations. ENTRY RULES Enter trades on the 15M, 1H or 4H timeframes. Enter a trade only when price retraces to a 50% or 60% retracement level and closes above (for  BUY) or below (for SELL) the level with a candlestick pattern. Enter more trades with new valid entry signals if trend is still valid  and if current positions are at zero risk. EXIT RULES Place stop loss below (for BUY) or above (for SELL) previous support or resistance levels. Place  take profit at 3 R TRADE MANAGEMENT RULES Take stop loss to breakeven when profits hit 0.8R to 1R. Trail the stop loss after profits hit 2R.
