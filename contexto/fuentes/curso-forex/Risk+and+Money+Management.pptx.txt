[1] RISK / MONEY MANAGEMENT Risk can be defined as a situation involving exposure to danger. Risk / Money management in forex trading, is the process of identifying, assessing and controlling threats to your trading capital and earnings. 5 very important Forex trading risk and money management techniques and concepts we’ll be looking at in this course include: Risk Avoidance Stoploss Trailing stops Breakeven Correlation Position sizing
[2] STOPLOSS, TRAILING STOP AND BREAKEVEN A stop loss is a type of order in forex trading designed to limit losses from a trade. A trailing stop is a special type of trade order that moves relative to price fluctuations. When the price goes up, it drags the trailing stop along with it, but when the price stops going up, the  stop loss price remains at the level it was dragged to. Breakeven is the point at which your trade neither makes nor loses money. It is also known as the price at which you have entered into a trading position.
[3] CORRELATION IN FOREX TRADING Currency correlation in forex is a positive or negative relationship between two separate currency pairs. A positive correlation means that two currency pairs move in positive directions, and a negative correlation means that they move in opposite directions . Correlation can make you double your risk in the market without even knowing it.
[4] POSITION SIZING Position sizing is basically the part of your trading, that tells you how many lots or contracts to buy or sell in any given trade. Your position sizing is Very important, in order to be successful in trading the forex market. No matter how good your strategy is, with poor position sizing, you will eventually lose your entire trading capital. Your position sizing helps you prepare for losses (which are inevitable), minimize them and recover your account more easily. Your position sizing helps you determine: Your risk per single trade and total risk on your account. How many lots to buy or sell Your expected returns.
[5] POSITION SIZING CALCULATION In order to calculate your position size, you have to: Determine your maximum risk per trade. (Recommended risk per trade is 1 to 3 percent of your trading capital per trade). Determine your account Size. Determine your  stoploss  size in pips. Determine the pip value per 1 standard lot (10usd for most pairs).

[7] RISK AND MONEY MANAGEMENT GOLDEN RULES Always make sure you avoid low quality trades. Always risk a fixed percentage of your trading capital. – 1 to 3 percent recommended. Always predefine a maximum amount you’re willing to risk losing if your trade goes wrong. Always use a stop loss order. Always predetermine your profit exit strategy, and trade management technique. Always ensure a Risk to reward ratio of at least 1 : 1.5 on every trade enter. Always trade correlated pairs with care. Do not enter a multiple trades on correlated pairs except if one of the trades have been taken to zero risk or your maximum risk per trade is divided between the trades.
