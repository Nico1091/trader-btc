[1] Spot Price, Bid and Ask Prices and Spreads SPOT PRICE:  The spot price is the current exchange rate at which a currency pair can be bought or sold in the forex market. It is basically the quote price for any given currency pair. BID PRICE:  The bid price is the price at which a forex trader sells a currency pair.  ASK PRICE:  The ask price is the price at which a forex trader buys a currency pair.  SPREAD:  The spread is the difference between the Ask price and the Bid price. The spread is what your broker charges you, when ever you buy or sell a currency pair. So if you initiate a Sell trade with your broker, your broker sells the currency pair for you at the bid price, and if you initiate a buy trade, your broker buys the currency trade for you at the Ask price and  the  difference between the two prices is the spread, which is the profit your broker makes off your trade.
