[1] CANDLESTICK PATTERNS A candlestick pattern is an arrangement of 0ne or more candlesticks in a chart, used by traders to identify high probability trading opportunities. Forex candlestick patterns are a form of chart analysis used by Forex traders to identify potential trading  opportunities, based on historical priced data. They must not be used in isolation, but in conjunction with other forms of technical and fundamental analysis. Some Forex candlestick patterns we’ll be learning about include: Engulfing patterns Inside bar ( Harami ) Spinning tops Tweezer  top and bottom. Doji Three white soldier and three black crows Morning and Evening star Hammer and Inverted Hammer Hanging man and Shooting Star.
[2] ENGULFING PATTERNS In a Bullish Engulfing pattern, a bearish candle is completely covered by a bullish candle that follows it. This signifies a possible reversal to the upward direction In a Bearish Engulfing pattern, a bullish candle is completely covered by a bearish candle that follows it. This signifies a possible reversal to the downward direction.
[3] INSIDE BAR (HARAMI) PATTERNS In a Bullish Inside bar, a big bearish candle is followed by a small bullish candle, with its body totally covered by the body of the big bearish candle. This signifies a possible reversal to the upward direction In a Bearish Inside bar, a big bullish candle is followed by a small bearish candle, with its body totally covered by the body of the big bullish candle. This signifies a possible reversal to the downward direction
[4] SPINNING TOP PATTERN A spinning top is formed when a candlestick has a long wick both above and below a narrow body. This candlestick pattern signifies indecision in the market, and the market could move in any direction.
[5] TWEEZER TOP AND BOTTOM PATTERNS A  tweezer  top occurs when the highs of two candlesticks occur at almost or exactly the same level following an upward move. This signifies a possible reversal to the  downward direction. A  tweezer  bottom occurs when the lows of two candlesticks occur at almost or exactly the same level following a downward move. This signifies a possible reversal to the upward direction.
[6] DOJI PATTERNS A  Doji  pattern has its open and close prices  equal (or almost exactly equal). The body appears as a very thin line. The are four main types of  doji  patterns namely: LONG-LEGGED DOJI – Which signifies indecision GRAVESTONE DOJI – Which signifies a possible reversal depending on where it’s found DRAGONFLY DOJI – Which signifies a possible reversal depending on where it’s found FOUR-PRICE DOJI – Which signifies indecision in the market
[7] THREE WHITE SOLDIERS PATTERN The three white soldiers pattern appears after an extended downtrend and small consolidation.  This signifies a possible major reversal to the upward direction. Qualities of Three white soldiers include: A bullish candle after a downward move. Another bullish candle with bigger body than the first and little to no upper wick. A third bullish candle with a body at least the size of the second one and little to no wick.
[8] THREE BLACK CROWS PATTERN The three black crows pattern appears after an extended uptrend and small consolidation.  This signifies a possible major reversal to the downward direction. Qualities of Three black crows include: A bearish candle after an upward move. Another bearish candle with bigger body than the first and little to no upper wick. A third bearish candle with a body at least the size of the second one and little to no wick.
[9] MORNING STAR PATTERN A morning star pattern plays out as a market hits a point of indecision after an extended downward movement. It consists  of three candlesticks: A bearish candle with a large body (which is part of the downtrend) A candle with a short body (Indicating that the bulls are getting in) A bullish candle with a tall body (confirming that a reversal has begun)
[10] EVENING  STAR  PATTERN An evening star pattern plays out as a market hits a point of indecision after an extended upward movement. It consists  of three candlesticks: A  bullish  candle with a large body (which is part of the uptrend) A candle with a short body (Indicating that the bears are getting in) A bearish candle with a tall body (confirming that a reversal has begun)
[11] HAMMER PATTERN If a Hammer is formed after an extended downward move, the traders believe it signifies a possible reversal or temporal pullback to the upside. A hammer has a long lower wick which is two to three times longer than the body, and has little to no upper wick.
[12] INVERTED HAMMER PATTERNS If an Inverted  h ammer is formed after an extended downward move, the traders believe it signifies a possible reversal or temporal pullback to the upside. An Inverted hammer has a long upper wick which is two to three times longer than the body, and has little to no lower wick.
[13] HANGING MAN PATTERN A Hanging man pattern is similar to a hammer pattern only that it appears after an uptrend unlike the hammer. It’s taken as a sign that selling sentiment is growing against the buyers and therefore a reversal may be coming soon.
[14] SHOOTING STAR PATTERNS A Shooting stat pattern is similar to an inverted hammer pattern only that it appears after an uptrend unlike the inverted hammer. It’s taken as a sign that selling sentiment is growing against the buyers and therefore a reversal may be coming soon.
