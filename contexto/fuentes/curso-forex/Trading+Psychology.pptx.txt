[1] TRADING PSYCHOLOGY Trading psychology is the most important part of forex trading because money is a very emotional thing and your emotions tend to completely come in play when you trade the forex market with real money. Your emotions can prevent you from doing the right things you’ve learnt, cause you to do the wrong things and cause you to do what is logically wrong. You psychology as a trader accounts for about 50% of what it takes for you  t o be successful as a trader. You can create  rules  to help keep your emotions at check. These rules can be part of your trading plan. Common emotional factors that affect us as traders include fear, Greed, Frustration, Hope, Lack of discipline.
[2] COMMON EMOTIONAL MISTAKES BY LOSING TRADERS They get too greedy and increase their lot sizes in order to make more money without realizing they are increasing their risks. They rush into new trades after some losses, in order to win back their lost money quickly. They refuse to close losing trades or use stop losses, with the hope that the market will turn around in their favor. They fear to profits already gained therefore close trades with small profits instead of allowing the trades to hit their target based on their strategy rules. They fear to take the next high probability trades because they lost a couple of trades previously. They fail to stick consistently to a specific risk per trade. They focus on how much money they make or lose instead of just following their strategy to see how it plays out in the long run. They keep changing and modifying their strategies after they’ve experienced some series of losses. They allow themselves to be emotionally affected by losing trades. They always want to have revenge on the market.
[3] SOME EMOTIONAL TRADING ADVICE Focus on being consistent with your strategy instead of always winning your trade because you can’t always win every trade, but consistently following your strategy will ensure you make money in the long run. Always take action when you see a high probability setup or signal. Accept losses as part of the game of trading. Accept them, but keep them as low as possible. Always have a maximum amount to lose a day and when you hit that amount in losses, close the computer or phone for the day and do something else. Only trade with money you can afford to lose. Do not borrow to trade. Be patient and ONLY wait for high probability setups. Do not dwell on your mistakes. Learn from them and move on.
