[1] SUPPORT AND RESISTANCE LEVELS A Support level describes a price level where a downtrend pauses due to an increased demand of an asset or currency. A Resistance level describes a price level where an uptrend pauses due to a decreased demand of an asset or currency.
[2] SUPPORT AND RESISTANCE LEVELS A Previous support level becomes a resistance level and a previous resistance level becomes a support level.
