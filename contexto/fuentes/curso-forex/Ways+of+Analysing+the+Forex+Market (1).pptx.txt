[1] FOREX MARKET ANALYSIS Forex market analysis is the study of a currency pair in order to determine whether to buy, sell, or wait on trading the currency pair.
[2] TECHNICAL ANALYSIS Forex Technical Analysis is a range of techniques used to try and forecast future price movements of a currency pair, completely by using historical price movements and patterns. Trend Law Price Patterns Indicators Candlestick Patterns Price Action Volume Support and Resistance Levels Supply and Demand Zones
[3] FUNDAMENTAL ANALYSIS Forex Fundamental Analysis is the process of studying the impact of political, economic and social factors on the relative value of a currency. Fundamental analysis helps forex traders identify primary drivers of a currency’s intrinsic value and this helps traders craft informed decisions on whether to buy or sell a currency. Fundamental analysis is more of an umbrella which covers the factors affecting the price fluctuations of a currency, outside of chart analysis. Overall state of the economy Interest rates Employment rate GDP(Gross Domestic Product) International trade Manufacturing Pandemics, Wars, Natural and Man made disasters etc.
