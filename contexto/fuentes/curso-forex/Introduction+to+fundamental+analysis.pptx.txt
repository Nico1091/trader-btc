[1] Introduction to fundamental analysis Fundamental analysis just like Technical analysis is a method of analyzing the financial markets, with the purpose of forecasting price movements. Forex fundamental analysis focuses on the overall state of the economy and researches various factors including interest rates, employment rate, GDP, international trade and  manufacturing geopolitics,  as well as their relative impact on the value of the national currency they relate to . Technical Analysis focus on the Charts (Price action, Indicators, Trends  etc ) while Fundamental Analysis focus on every other thing other than the price. In Fundamental Analysis , we  primarily use Economic Indicators to understand the state of the economy of countries or jurisdiction whose currencies we want to trade. Fundamental analysts track these economic indicators using Economic Calendars and draw conclusions by comparing the values of these economic indicators, with the historical and forecasted values, by comparing them with each other, and by comparing them with time. Fundamental analysts also pay attention to Central Banks because they are responsible for monitoring the economy, and making policies that affect the economies of their countries.
[2] TERMINOLOGIES IN FUNDAMENTAL ANALYSIS An economy encompasses all of the activities related to the production, consumption, and trade of goods and services in  a  nation or  jurisdiction. ECONOMY In Economics, supply simply refers to the total amount of specified product or service that is available to customers. While demand, refers to the consumer’s desire to buy the product or service. SUPPLY AND DEMAND Commodities are naturally occurring materials or goods that are collected and processed for use in human activity – such as oil, sugar and precious metals. COMMODITY Inflation is simply a rise in the prices of commodities. During inflation, money loses its value because there is less supply of goods and service and more supply of money in the economy. Deflation is a fall in the prices of commodities. During deflation, the purchasing power of money increases. Inflation and deflation need to be at a balanced level for the economy to move well.
