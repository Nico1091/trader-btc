[1] INDICATORS Indicators are tools used by Forex traders in technical analysis, which help traders forecast and confirm price changes and direction in the market. Indicators are computer programs developed with mathematical calculations, to act based on price action, volume, momentum and other important metrics. Indicators have two main functions; to Confirm and to Predict. Indicators are lagging systems. 4 TYPES OF INDICATORS include Trend Indicators Volume indicators Volatility indicators Momentum indicators (Know as Oscillators)
[2] TREND  INDICATORS Trend indicators help traders identify the strength and direction of the trend on  the timeframe they are placed on. Examples of trend indicators include: Moving Average MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence) Parabolic SAR ADMI (Average Direction Movement)
[3] VOLUME  INDICATORS These category of indicators are used to determine if the volume behind a particular movement is high or low. High volume typically shows that a pair is being actively traded. They are used by a lot of traders in spotting reversals. Major reversals in a trend usually carry high volumes. Some examples of volume indicators are listed below: Money flow index Chaikin  money flow Rate of change (ROC) Ease of movement On-balance volume.
[4] VOLATILITY  INDICATORS These category of indicators are used to determine the volatility in the market. Volatility is the rate at which the prices of a currenc y pair or asset changes. If the prices are changing rapidly, then it means there is high volatility and if not rapidly, it means there’s low volatility. Some  examples of volatility indicators include: Bollinger bands Average true range (ATR) Envelops
[5] MOMENTUM  INDICATORS Also known as Oscillators, these indicators help tell traders about the momentum change in the market. They calculate the momentum of the move and then plot it on a graph of 0 to 100. 0 showing that the pair or asset is over sold and 100 over bought. Momentum indicators help traders find reversal points in the market and also help confirm the strength or momentum of a move. Some examples of these indicators include: Relative strength index (RSI) Stochastic indicator Commodity channel index (CCI)
[6] COMBINING MULTIPLE INDICATORS One mistake a lot of beginner traders make when starting out is being too dependent on indicators, therefore using too many indicators on their charts and selecting indicators randomly. By doing so, they end up becoming more confused and more frustrated. SOME ADVICE WHEN COMBINING & USING INDICATORS Do not combine indicators of the same type because they give similar information except if the strategy is very specific about combining like indicators. When combining indicators, use indicators from different categories, that show different information. Do not use too many indicators. 2 to 4 is enough. Understand that losses are part of forex trading so instead of trying to eliminate losses with too many indicators, traders should instead use proper risk / trade management to minimize losses and maximize wins. Some good indicator combination are Trend indicator + Momentum Indicator, Volatility Indicator + Momentum Indicator, Trend indicator + Volume Indicator.
