[1] Currency pairs A currency pair is a quote of one currency against another currency. This is a form of comparism between two currencies.  Eg . EUR/USD, GBP/USD, USD/CAD, EUR/AUD, GBP/NZD etc. There are thousands of currency pairs in the Forex market. BASE CURRENCY: The First currency listed. It is always 1 QUOTE CURRENCY: The Second currency listed. It is the exchange rate between the Base and Quote currencies Each Currency in the world can be identified by a 3 letter code know as its ISO Currency Code. ISO = International Organization for Standardization
