[1] S essions in forex trading Even though the Forex market runs for 24 hours in a day, you have to understand that all major market, in different parts of the world have their peculiar timing. And their timing is what sessions in forex is all about. The Forex market can be broken into 4 or 5 major sessions, in relation with the 4 major markets in the world. One important thing to note is that the peak trading hours or most active trading hours are usually experienced in periods of intersection between different sessions. And during a currency’s session, that currency tends to be stronger, because that country or region’s economy is most active at that period. This is however not always the case as this is also subjective to fundamental and technical analysis. THE 4 MAJOR FOREX MARKET SESSIONS ARE: Sydney Session (Australia) The Tokyo Session (Japan) The London Session (England) The  Newyork  Session (USA)
